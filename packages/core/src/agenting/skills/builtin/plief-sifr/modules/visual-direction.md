# Visual Direction — art direction as a causal thesis

Use after structure settles, when direction is genuinely open or a reference must become an original language. Freeze the decision in `design-dna.json`; for full visual work validate with `engines/ir_validate.py --dna <path> --strict-visual`.

Art direction is not a style word. It connects `product truth -> intended perception -> formal laws -> implementation -> proof`.

## ArtDirection brief

Before components, commit these DesignDNA fields:

```text
experience_archetype  dominant product behavior the visuals must serve
visual_thesis         one causal sentence, not “premium/clean/modern”
composition_law       grid, focal path, overlap/asymmetry, density and negative-space logic
type_roles             display/body/control/data voices, measure and responsive behavior
chroma_budget          dominance, accent scarcity and semantic exceptions
material_light_law     surface physics, transparency, texture, depth and light direction
asset_world            source, subject, crop, camera/illustration treatment and consistency
motion_character       tempo, spatial model, motifs and interruption character
signature_move         exactly one product-specific memorable relationship
counter_default        nearest plausible generic pattern deliberately refused
```

Record evidence and risks: readability/contrast, density mismatch, responsive fragility, font availability/licensing/payload, asset reality, transparency, motion/vestibular load, media/GPU cost and design-system divergence.

## Direction procedure

1. Freeze common truth: every option solves the same product problem, IA, content, states, viewports, accessibility obligations and invariants.
2. Derive dimensions with actual freedom (editorial↔tool-like, restrained↔expressive, geometric↔organic, sparse↔dense, polished↔raw, static↔kinetic). Coordinate them into a thesis; never sample random style coordinates.
3. Use `../references/atlas.md` only now to select/reject a dominant visual vocabulary. Named languages are shorthand after the thesis, never the thesis itself. A hybrid needs one obvious dominant grammar.
4. When comparison can change a decision, create 2–4 coherent theses. First is the strongest conservative fit; wider options remain credible. Different fonts or colors on identical composition are one option. Render options with identical viewport, copy, state and controls when capability exists; do not compare moodboard prose under unequal evidence.
5. Prove distinctness after logo removal, accent neutralization, grayscale, effects-off and same-content substitution. If only the decoration changes, the direction is not distinct.
6. Select one winner with its dominant tradeoff and falsification condition. One disqualifying accessibility, product or runtime risk beats an aggregate score. Do not end with “all are valid.”
7. Refine the chosen thesis; do not casually hybridize rejected options into an unreviewed extra direction.

## Formal discipline

- Composition owns identity before effects: decide silhouette, anchor, visual path, scale contrast, density and whitespace tension.
- Typography is architecture: a distinctive display face cannot rescue generic hierarchy; a single family with width/weight/optical axes may be more authored than an arbitrary pair.
- Color is relational: choose dominance and accent scarcity; test semantic states and actual dynamic backgrounds.
- Material and light need physics: say what floats, reflects, absorbs, emits or refracts and why. Glass, glow, grain and shadow cannot all be the hierarchy.
- Asset world is a system, not an image search result. Product evidence outranks decorative stock; inconsistent camera/light/crop breaks the world.
- Minimalism requires stronger type, spacing, alignment, content and material precision because fewer decisions carry more identity. Maximalism requires an explicit hierarchy and repetition system; “more effects” is not one.

Output: DesignDNA plus a short coherence proof linking at least three formal laws to product pressure. Do not write moodboard prose that has no implementation consumer.
